⚠ Important correction noted during research: The history page originally stated the Emirate was founded in the "early 19th century." Research confirms the Biu Kingdom was founded significantly earlier — around 1535 CE (mid-16th century) by Yamta-ra-Wala (Yamta the Great), with the Kingdom becoming fully established around 1670 CE in the reign of Mari Watila Tampta. The formal title of "Emir" was introduced in 1920 when the British acknowledged Mai Ari Dogo as the first Emir. These corrected dates are documented in multiple sources below and should be updated in the final client history when they confirm their preferred version.

The Biu Plateau — Geography & Geology

Sources confirming the volcanic plateau, elevation, and terrain

Smithsonian / Global Volcanism Program

Biu Plateau — Basaltic Volcanic Lava Flows; Cinder Cones

"The Biu Plateau in NE Nigeria is composed of basaltic lava flows. A large number of recent cinder cones with well-defined craters are aligned in a NNW-SSE direction over a distance of 50 km. The cinder cones and lava flows often contain abundant peridotite xenoliths."
Smithsonian Institution — Global Volcanism Program, Biu Plateau entry

Tila Lake — Sacred Crater Lake

Sources confirming Tila Lake, the sacred crocodiles, and its status as a tourist site

Tila Lake — Crater Lake in Extinct Volcano; Sacred Crocodiles

"The crocodiles of Tila Lake, a crater lake in an extinct volcano measuring 1,800 feet (550 m), just southwest of the town, are sacred to Biu's traditional rulers."

"The crocodile is the sign that is being used to represent Biu Kingdom because of the Tila Lake and Kwadankir Pool... Tila Lake has always been considered as the unfathomable lakes by the locals. This Tila Lake is full of crocodiles which is said to be creamier colour than those in the surrounding rivers... something exciting about these crocodiles is that nobody understands what they feed on because there are no fish or frogs in the lake."

Economy & Resources of Biu

Sources confirming agriculture, trade, and mineral resources

Wikipedia — Biu, Nigeria

Mixed Agricultural Economy; Mining Industry; Crops

"The LGA is mostly located in the northern Guinea savannah agroecological zone. The economy is mixed agricultural, based on herding cattle, goats, sheep, horses, and donkeys and farming sorghum, millet, maize, cowpea, and cotton. There is a small mining industry in Biu, with iron ore, gravel, magnesite, uranium, feldspar, topaz, mica, granite, aquamarine, nephelite, and salt being extracted."
