HOW EMAIL COLLECTION NOW WORKS
───────────────────────────────
Every subscribe/registry form across the site now does TWO things:

  1. FIRESTORE — saves subscriber to "subscribers" collection
     → View in Firebase Console → Firestore → subscribers
     → Full details: name, email, LGA, location, occupation, etc.
     → Admin dashboard → Subscribers tab → Export CSV anytime

  2. MAILCHIMP — adds email to your Mailchimp mailing list
     → From Mailchimp you can send bulk newsletters/announcements
     → Free plan: 500 contacts, 1,000 emails/month

FORMS CONNECTED
───────────────
  ✓ index.html         → Registry popup modal
  ✓ news.html          → Subscribe form
  ✓ events.html        → Subscribe form
  ✓ join-registry.html → Full registry form (saves all details)

STEP 1 — CREATE YOUR FREE MAILCHIMP ACCOUNT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Go to: https://mailchimp.com
2. Click "Sign Up Free"
3. Enter your email, username, password → Create Account
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete the setup wizard (organisation name: "Biu Forum")

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 2 — GET YOUR MAILCHIMP FORM URL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. In Mailchimp → click "Audience" in the left menu
2. Click "Signup forms"
3. Click "Embedded forms"
4. Click "Generate code"
5. Look for the <form> tag in the generated code
6. Copy the URL inside action="..." — it looks like:

   https://yourdomain.us1.list-manage.com/subscribe/post?u=XXXX&id=XXXX

   That full URL is your MAILCHIMP_URL.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 3 — PASTE THE URL INTO YOUR WEBSITE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Open the file: assets/js/site.js
2. Find this line near the top (line ~116):

   const MAILCHIMP_URL = 'YOUR_MAILCHIMP_FORM_ACTION_URL_HERE';

3. Replace YOUR_MAILCHIMP_FORM_ACTION_URL_HERE with your real URL:

   const MAILCHIMP_URL = 'https://yourdomain.us1.list-manage.com/subscribe/post?u=XXXX&id=XXXX';

4. Save the file
5. Re-upload to Netlify

That's it! Mailchimp is now connected.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
STEP 4 — UPDATE FIRESTORE RULES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The updated Firestore rules are in FIREBASE_RULES.txt
Paste them in Firebase Console → Firestore → Rules → Publish

HOW TO VIEW YOUR SUBSCRIBERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

METHOD 1 — Admin Dashboard (easiest):
  → Go to yoursite.netlify.app/admin
  → Log in with your Firebase credentials
  → Click "Subscribers" in the left sidebar
  → See the live list with names, emails, LGAs, locations
  → Click "Export CSV" to download the full list as a spreadsheet

METHOD 2 — Firebase Console:
  → console.firebase.google.com → biu-forum
  → Firestore Database → subscribers collection
  → Click any document to see full details

METHOD 3 — Mailchimp:
  → mailchimp.com → Audience → Contacts
  → See all email subscribers, send bulk emails

SENDING A NEWSLETTER TO YOUR SUBSCRIBERS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Go to mailchimp.com → Campaigns → Create Campaign
2. Choose "Email"
3. Choose your audience (Biu Forum list)
4. Design your email using their templates
5. Click Send — all subscribers receive it instantly

Free plan allows 1,000 sends per month.
