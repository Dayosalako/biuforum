Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a news story?

Use the contact form and select "News Submission / Tip" as the subject. Include as many details as possible.

How do I add a dignitary to the Emirs page?

Select "Dignitary Profile Submission" and include the person's name, title, dates of service, biography, and if possible a photo.

Is Biu Forum free to use?

Yes. The platform is completely free for all Biu citizens, now and always.
